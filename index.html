About Abby Medics

Abby Medics is a leading digital healthcare platform in Uganda, connecting patients with qualified medical professionals from top hospitals across the country.

Our mission is to make quality healthcare accessible, affordable, and convenient for everyone through innovative technology solutions.

Founded in 2020, we've grown to serve thousands of patients with our network of over 500 doctors and 50 partner hospitals.
